About This Episode

In Episode 5 of Find Your Clarity, host Khawar Shahzad and Babur Ayub look at seduction beyond romance: the art of attracting attention, influencing people and holding power in everyday life, from offices and business deals to friendships and family.

Babur Ayub draws on his 25+ years in corporate Pakistan to explain why people are drawn to "dark energy", how marketing links a product with something admired, why too much availability and over-niceness reduce your weight in a room, and why a person who listens, controls their reactions and speaks last tends to be the most powerful one present.

The episode closes with how to build real inner strength: learning from failures and hardships, practising patience, and finding people who appreciate and motivate you.

Key Takeaways

  • Seduction is not only about romance. It is the art of gaining attention and influence, and it shows up in sales, offices, friendships and families.
  • Keeping part of the picture unsaid keeps people curious and makes them think for themselves. When everything is told, the curiosity is gone.
  • We are drawn to the "dark side" because every person has one, and it carries curiosity and depth. Superhero and supervillain films are the example given.
  • Marketing works by linking a product with something people admire. The tyre-advertisement example shows why the link matters more than the product itself.
  • The same question framed differently gets a different answer. "What is the best part of your work which you secretly enjoy?" opens more than "What do you do?"
  • Too much availability reduces the weight of your presence, and being over-nice or flattering drains your value. Flatterers also get exposed quickly.
  • The most powerful person in the room controls their reactions, listens, speaks last, and does not need other people's acceptance.
  • Real inner strength comes from experience, hardships and patience, and from people who appreciate and motivate you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does seduction mean in this episode?

Babur Ayub explains that seduction is not only about sex or romance. It is the art of gaining attraction and attention and of influencing people, for example in sales, interviews and offices, often by keeping part of the story open so that people stay curious.

Why are people drawn to dark energy?

According to the episode, every individual has a dark side that they don't express, so we associate ourselves with it when we see it in someone else. The dark side also carries more curiosity and depth, which makes it more attractive, as in superhero and supervillain films.

Why does being too nice make you weak?

Too much availability reduces the weight of your presence, and being over-nice or flattering makes you lose value. The episode says to stay balanced: be nice, but avoid being extra kind, extra benevolent or flattering, since flatterers get exposed quickly.

How can you become the most powerful person in the room?

The guest's answer is to control your reactions, listen, speak last and keep quiet when you don't need to speak, and to not depend on others' acceptance. A powerful person is there to make decisions rather than to make sense to everyone.

How do you build inner strength?

The episode points to experience: failures, tough times and hardships you learn from, together with patience and waiting for your turn. People who appreciate and motivate you, and with whom you can comfortably share your thoughts, are another source of strength.

Who is the guest in this episode?

Babur Ayub, a leader with 25+ years in Pakistan's retail and fashion industry, a lecturer at STEP IADM, a startup mentor and the author of "Management by Qur'an".
