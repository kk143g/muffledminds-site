About This Episode

In Episode 12 of Muffled Minds, host Khawar Shahzad sits down with Ali Rehman Khan, founder of Founders Klub and the innovator behind a water-saving nozzle. The episode opens in COMSATS Abbottabad in 2015, where Ali was the outlier student, last into class and first to leave, who failed his Calculus paper by just seven marks and was told by his teacher that he was good for nothing. Years later he returned to the same campus as a guest speaker and told that same teacher his words can break a life, or make one.

Ali tells his own journey: growing up in Bannu, an underprivileged, war-affected district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, a picky, outlier child who would not fit the mould; teaching himself graphic design; discovering entrepreneurship through JumpStart Pakistan; and inventing his nozzle from a bottle cap, winning the first SDG Bootcamp in 2018 and a 2 million PKR grant from the KP government. His nozzle, installed in guest houses and homes, has saved around 1.5 million litres of water.

The conversation then turns to the branding agency Designs Crunch, building Founders Klub to close the access gap for founders without capital, mentors or networks, family pressure and the log kya kahenge taboo, parental counselling for founders, what success really means, and his message to the youth: Pakistan is full of problems, and every problem is an opportunity, before the Fish Jar quick-fire round, including his craziest experience in Moscow.

About The Guest

Ali Rehman Khan
Founder of Founders Klub · Innovator

Ali Rehman Khan is the founder of Founders Klub, a community empowering underserved Pakistani founders, and the innovator behind a water-saving nozzle. He grew up in Bannu district in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, an underprivileged, war-affected and resource-deprived area, and calls himself an underrated, misunderstood polymath.

He studied software engineering at COMSATS Abbottabad, where he failed a Calculus paper by seven marks and was humiliated by his teacher, a turning point that pushed him to prove himself. He went on to win the first SDG Bootcamp in 2018 and a 2 million PKR grant from the KP government, founded the branding agency Designs Crunch, and introduced the first Startup Grind Bannu chapter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Ali Rehman Khan?

Ali Rehman Khan is the founder of Founders Klub, a community that gives underserved Pakistani founders access to capital, resources and mentorship, and the innovator behind a water-saving nozzle. He is from Bannu district in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, studied software engineering at COMSATS Abbottabad, and calls himself an underrated, misunderstood polymath.

What happened with his Calculus paper at COMSATS?

In 2015, in his second or third semester at COMSATS Abbottabad, Ali failed his Calculus paper by seven marks. When he checked, two or three questions had not been marked at all. When he asked his teacher to recheck, the teacher threw his paper at him, told him to get out, and said he was good for nothing and could not achieve anything in life.

How does his water-saving nozzle work?

The nozzle converts the normal flow of water into mist, millions of tiny droplets, with three modes. The mist mode saves 90 to 95 percent of water, and even the higher-flow modes save 70 to 85 percent. Installed in guest houses and homes, it has saved around 1.5 million litres of water.

What is Founders Klub?

Founders Klub is the community Ali created to reduce the digital and innovation gap for founders who have ideas but no access to capital, resources, opportunities or the right mentorship. It offers access to capital and resources, ready-to-use templates such as term sheets, and guidance on conferences and scaling globally.

What does Ali say success means?

He says success is leaving this world satisfied, with at least one person saying behind your back that you changed their life. He also quotes Alibaba: today is hard, tomorrow is harsh, and the day after tomorrow is the brightest of all, but most people give up on day two.
