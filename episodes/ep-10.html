About This Episode

In Episode 10 of Muffled Minds, host Khawar Shahzad sits down with Haji Muhammad Rafiq, self-made entrepreneur and owner of Four Seasons Footwear, who rose from the narrow streets of Gawalmandi to supplying shoes for Pakistan's top brands. The episode opens with the story that frames the whole conversation: a shy schoolboy who would not say what his father did, until his teacher told him never to say my father makes shoes, but to say my father is a shoe manufacturer, a shift in mindset that changed his life.

The journey runs from childhood in Gawalmandi, Azizul Islam School and principal Mian Syed Ahmed Iqbal, through Air Force selection and a return home as the eldest son, to the cricket-day money collection from Globe Shoes, Punjab Shoes and Rolex Shoes, and the building of Four Seasons Footwear from four boxes and eight samples to a showroom full of samples. He supplies Bata, Service, ECS, Metro and more, went to Dubai in 2003 and bought a house in MR Villas, and lives in Noor Mehal, the house his father liked, bought in 1984 for 60 lakh and now valued at more than a billion rupees.

The lessons are the heart of the episode: success is sukoon-e-qalb, peace of heart, not money or position; business runs on 60 percent honesty, 20 percent skill and 20 percent hard work, with spiritual upbringing alongside. He tells the 15-rupee tax story and how he now pays tax in millions, the Russian customer who offered him a factory, the bulb and the government, and his advice for parents and the next generation, before the Fish Jar question asks whether happiness happens only when you let it happen.

About The Guest

Haji Muhammad Rafiq
Owner of Four Seasons Footwear · Self-made entrepreneur

Haji Muhammad Rafiq is a self-made entrepreneur and shoe manufacturer from Gawalmandi, Lahore, and the owner of Four Seasons Footwear. He rose from the narrow streets of the inner city to manufacturing shoes for Pakistan's top footwear brands, including Bata and Service, and has spent decades building his business from handmade shoes to a multi-brand supply operation.

He grew up in Gawalmandi, on Railway Road, where his late father ran a small shoe workshop. He studied at a government school, was briefly selected for the Air Force before returning home as the eldest son, and started in the shoe trade with four boxes and eight samples, taking orders across Peshawar, Rawalpindi and beyond. He lives in Noor Mehal, the historic Gawalmandi house his father admired, and has five daughters and one son.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Haji Muhammad Rafiq?

Haji Muhammad Rafiq is a self-made entrepreneur and shoe manufacturer from Gawalmandi, Lahore, and the owner of Four Seasons Footwear. He rose from the narrow streets of the inner city to manufacturing shoes for Pakistan's top footwear brands, including Bata and Service, and has spent decades building his business from handmade shoes to a multi-brand supply operation.

What is Four Seasons Footwear?

Four Seasons Footwear is Haji Muhammad Rafiq's shoe manufacturing company. It supplies shoes to Bata, Service, ECS, Metro, Modela, Fit Rite, Imperial and English Shoes, and grew from four boxes and eight samples carried to Peshawar and Rawalpindi into a showroom full of samples, with a warehouse and showroom in Dubai as well.

What is the story behind Mera Baap Juty Banata Hai?

As a boy in sixth class at Azizul Islam School, Rafiq was ashamed to tell his classmates that his father made shoes, because he feared they would call him a cobbler. His principal, Mian Syed Ahmed Iqbal, called him aside and told him never to say my father makes shoes, but to say my father is a shoe manufacturer. That one sentence changed how he saw his father's work, and his own future.

What does Haji Rafiq say success is?

He says success is sukoon-e-qalb, peace of heart, not money or position. He says he is a king himself, because his heart is rich, and that whoever has peace of heart should understand he is successful. His formula for business is 60 percent honesty, 20 percent skill and 20 percent hard work, with spiritual upbringing alongside.

What is Noor Mehal?

Noor Mehal is the historic house in Gawalmandi where Haji Rafiq lives. His father admired it, and Rafiq bought it in 1984 for 60 lakh rupees, selling his house and car to raise the 40 lakh he had. A property dealer later valued it at more than a billion rupees, and Pervez Musharraf visited it in 1999.
