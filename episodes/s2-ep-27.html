About This Episode

In Episode 27 (Season 2) of the Muffled Minds Podcast, host Khawar Shahzad sits down with Faizan Malik, founder of Culture Shawq Entertainment Club, a stand-up comedy club in Pakistan. A mechanical engineer by degree, Faizan studied at Michigan State University in the USA, started doing stand-up at an open mic in a coffee shop in East Lansing, and after returning to Pakistan in 2023, started his own comedy club. The episode opens with a teaser of his sharpest lines, then traces the whole journey from engineering to comedy.

Faizan explains why he chose mechanical engineering, how comedy began as a healthy distraction that turned unhealthy, and how the name Culture Shawq came from the culture shock he felt going back and forth between Pakistan and America. He talks about starting a club from zero in a country with no bars, no third spaces and no infrastructure for stand-up, and about the mindset and resistance he faced, on stage and off. The conversation then turns to the stage drama artists who came before him, and to Aftab Iqbal, who took stage actors into commercial television, gave them family-friendly shows and movie roles, and had to move his show to the UAE when politics got involved.

The episode compares comedy in India and Pakistan, where India has bars, a third space and an entertainment industry tied to Bollywood, while Pakistan has no proper place for stand-up and an audience that often takes crowd work personally. It closes on comedy and mental health, with Faizan speaking openly about his own anxiety, insomnia medication and the stigma around antidepressants, and on the future of Culture Shawq, before a rapid-fire round on biryani, bombing on stage and scripted versus spontaneous comedy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Culture Shawq?

Culture Shawq Entertainment Club is a stand-up comedy club in Pakistan, founded by Faizan Malik after he returned from the USA. The name is a play on culture shock, spelled with a Q, from the shock he felt going back and forth between Pakistan and America.

How did Faizan Malik start doing stand-up comedy?

He started at an open mic at the Blue Owl Coffee Shop in East Lansing, USA, in the summer of 2019, while studying mechanical engineering at Michigan State University. He started writing jokes on a Monday, refined them on Tuesday and Wednesday, and performed at the open mic that Wednesday evening.

Why did Aftab Iqbal move his show to the UAE?

Faizan says Aftab Iqbal had to move his show to the UAE after political issues got involved, and then ran it remotely, with some of his actors in Pakistan and himself in the UAE.

What is the difference between comedy in India and Pakistan?

Faizan says India is far ahead infrastructurally, with bars and third spaces to perform in, while Pakistan has no proper place for stand-up. He also says Indian audiences accept crowd work and enjoy it, while Pakistani audiences often take it personally.

Does Faizan Malik talk about mental health in this episode?

Yes. He speaks openly about his own anxiety and insomnia medication, about the stigma around antidepressants and anti-anxiety medicine in Pakistan, and says mental health should be taken seriously, with a proper checkup.

Who is the guest in this episode?

The guest is Faizan Malik, founder of Culture Shawq Entertainment Club, a stand-up comedy club in Pakistan. A mechanical engineer by degree, he studied in the USA, started doing stand-up comedy in a coffee shop, and returned to Pakistan to start his own club.
