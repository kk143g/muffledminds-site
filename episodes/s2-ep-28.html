About This Episode

In Episode 28 (Season 2, Part 1) of the Muffled Minds Podcast, host Khawar Shahzad sits down with Dr. Aqeel Ahmed, Head of the MS and PhD Business Administration programs at UCP (University of Central Punjab). The episode opens with Dr. Aqeel as a 13-14 year old asthma patient watching aeroplanes and dreaming of sitting in one, and with the start of his career at 12 rupees a day. He states his vision in one line: he wants to become a kingmaker.

Dr. Aqeel traces his early life in Rahim Yar Khan and his first job as an office boy in 1986, his hijrat to Lahore for higher education in 1989, and his appointment as a Junior Clerk (BS-5) in 1993. In 1997 he ran a paan shop, gave tuition and sold fries — his mother's small shop made 78 rupees on its first day and reached thousands within five or six months, and the fries stall doubled as a place where he taught accounting entries to students. In 1998 he took the risk of an MBA, and in 2001 he moved with his family to Lahore.

The conversation then follows his M.Phil in Malaysia and the role of Qarz-e-Hasna (2004), his PhD in Malaysia (2009), his return to Pakistan in 2014 and his first teaching job there. He talks about how to pursue your dreams, the role of the University of Central Punjab, advice for youth on pursuing higher education, the role of a supervisor in research, and the difference between an MPhil and a PhD. The episode closes with the story of a PhD cancellation letter and his turn to the mosque, and Part 2 is announced for next week.
