About This Episode

Malik Aun Abbas, Director and CEO of LITSO Travel & Tours, joins Khawar Shahzad to talk about why Pakistani weddings have become so expensive, and why he believes the same money is better spent on a honeymoon and on travel. He has explored 50 plus countries, and his company's motto is Life Is To Serve Others.

The conversation covers the pressure of dowry, one-dish rules and the violation charges wedding halls impose, the way social media raises expectations, and the scams that trap people who want to go abroad, from fake agents and the Dubai iqama loan fraud to the donkey route that puts lives at risk. He also explains why so many Pakistani visa applications are rejected, from dishonest applications and no travel history to the bans on travellers from certain cities.

He shares stories from his own travels, a hitchhiker he hosted in Lahore, Chinese teams digging for jade in the north, a professor in Athens who recognised him as Punjabi from history books, and horse meat in Central Asia, and ends with his favourite and most overrated destinations, and advice for young entrepreneurs: consistency, discipline and trust in Allah.

  • Dowry, one-dish rules and the violation charges wedding halls impose
  • How social media and TikTok trends raise wedding expectations
  • Travel as an investment, and what the Quran says about travelling
  • Why Pakistani visa applications get rejected, and the bans on some cities
  • The Dubai iqama loan scam and how agents exploit job visas
  • The donkey route to Europe, and why parents should not risk their children
  • Funny and memorable travel stories, from Athens to Central Asia
  • Favourite, overrated and underrated destinations, and advice for young entrepreneurs
