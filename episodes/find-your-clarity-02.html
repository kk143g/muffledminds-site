About This Episode

In Episode 2 of Find Your Clarity, host Khawar Shahzad talks with Babur Ayub about the journey of mankind — from caves to AI. They ask how humans began, what the first questions were that made us human, and how we reached 2026, an age in which artificial intelligence changes almost every third day, while the human brain is still largely the one built for life in the wild.

Babur traces the journey step by step: the first question, 'Who am I?', and the second, 'Who created me?'; the invention of fire, clothes, shoes and rules; language and storytelling, the most refined and most powerful beginning of the human career; writing on slates, trees and wood, and paper invented in China more than 5,000 years ago; the printing press of 1440, which its maker was sentenced to death for, and which the Ottoman Empire refused to adopt; the Industrial Revolution, which gave us denim jeans for workers and shorter skirts for the bicycle; and the demand for real deliverables for the welfare of mankind, from the train to modern transport.

The episode ends in the present: Mohenjo-daro, a planned city 5,000 years old with covered drains and proper bathrooms, shows that planning is not new — but today the flood of fake information on social media and AI-generated videos leaves people scattered and unsure what to believe. Babur's answer is a filter inside yourself: trust direct, subjective experience, check what fits your own experience and background, and remember that truth means a different thing to every person.

  • The printing press was made almost in 1440, and the man who made it was given the death sentence — and the Ottoman Empire refused the latest technology, which Babur believes is one of the reasons for its downfall.
  • Allah told us to progress, to move forward and to explore — the Quran says, 'I have created you upon my own reflection.'

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the first question that made humans human?

Babur Ayub says the first question was 'Who am I?' — the question that declared the species human. The second question was 'Who created me?', and from there came the idea of a purpose, rules, tribes and religion.

Why did the Ottoman Empire fall behind?

According to Babur Ayub, the Ottomans refused to adopt the printing press and the latest technology of their time. He believes that refusal is one of the reasons for the downfall of the Ottoman Empire. The Christians also gave the death sentence to the man who invented the printing press.

Why were jeans invented?

Jeans were made for industrial workers. Levi Strauss invented denim, a long-lasting fabric, because a worker's clothes were destroyed after four or five washes in the factories, where there were oils and iron furnaces and the hands got dirty.

What does Mohenjo-daro show about ancient planning?

Mohenjo-daro and Harappa were among the very first planned cities, about 5,000 years old. They had a planned housing society, a gutter system, proper bathrooms, and separate utensils for breakfast and lunch, with natural air reaching the rooms.

How can we deal with misinformation and AI-generated videos?

Babur Ayub says we need to develop a filter inside ourselves: believe the person with direct experience — the one who burnt his hand in the fire will tell you the truth about it — and check whether information fits your own experience and background, instead of passing on whatever you are told.
