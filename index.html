Our Story

We study how the world's most extraordinary people think

Muffled Minds is a Pakistani podcast for people who want to live and love better. Host @khawarrism sits down with real heroes, entrepreneurs, psychologists, teachers, comedians and everyday people with powerful stories, in Urdu & English, to uncover the mindsets, habits and lessons behind how they got where they are.

It's pure learning: how to build, grow, heal and think clearer, for a generation tired of pretending everything is fine.

Because success starts in the mind, we also talk openly about mental health, depression and anxiety, the struggles people carry in silence, and the taboos that keep us quiet.

New episodes every Thursday & Saturday. Subscribe and find your clarity.
